Into the Woods

A personality quiz told as a short story.

Chapter 1

Something big and brown moves out of the corner of your eye. It's a bear, maybe thirty feet off the trail, and it's looking right at you. What do you do?

Chapter 1½

The bear's claws catch your arm. You grab the bear spray your friend packed in your bag, and you run. Which way do you go?

Chapter 2

The bear is gone, and so are your friends. You're lost in the woods, and your phone is about to die. What do you do?

Chapter 3

Your phone dies as soon as you try to turn it on... What do you do?

Chapter 4

The sun is setting, and it's getting a bit cold. There's a big fallen tree, and it seems like a great shelter for now.

Chapter 5

The sun is gone. Your stomach growls, and your throat is so dry it hurts. What do you do?

Chapter 5½

About twenty minutes later, the bark on the tree starts to breathe. The stars leave little trails when you move your head. Your hands look like they belong to someone else. What now?

Chapter 6

Something moves at the edge of the trees. A dark shape. Then another. They're tall, and they're standing very, very still. They're watching you.

Chapter 7

Then a voice drifts out of the dark. It sounds exactly like your friend. "Hey... come here. Come away from there. We found the way out." But your friend has never said your name like that. Flat, like it's being read off a card.

Chapter 8

You're out in the open now. It's pitch black, the cold bites through your jacket, and you have no idea which way your shelter is. Which way do you go?

Chapter 8

The woods go quiet. Sunrise is hours away, and the cold is settling into your bones. How do you get through the night?

Chapter 9

A ranger wraps a crinkly foil blanket around your shoulders and hands you a cup of hot chocolate. "So," she says. "What happened out there?" What's the first thing you say?
